Chile Mining Ops Agent

A tool-calling agent over real public mining data (a flotation plant, its instrument events, and Chilean public procurement), measured on 100 labeled queries with Qwen2.5-3B running locally on CPU.

With raw pesos the model miscopied amounts in 21 of 25 procurement answers (×10 slips, misplaced separators). Returning the same amounts in millions of CLP fixed most of them without touching the model. Two failures no number check catches remain: for months with no data it silently answers about another month, and it sometimes reports hours as days.
